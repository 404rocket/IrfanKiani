Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how M S Irfan Kiani ("we," "us," or "the business") collects, uses, and protects information that you provide when using our website or contacting us.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when contacting us, including your name, contact details, and any information included in your message, brief, or inquiry. We also collect basic technical information (IP address, browser type, pages visited) automatically through standard web server logs.

2. How we use your information

Information you provide is used to respond to your inquiries, deliver services you have requested, maintain business records as required by law, and improve our website. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Business communications

If you contact us by email, phone, or another business channel, we may use the contact details you provide to respond to your inquiry, discuss project details, schedule calls, or provide updates related to a requested professional engagement. We do not use contact details for unrelated mass marketing or sell contact information to third parties.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share your information with third parties except where required to deliver a service you have engaged us for, where required by law, or with service providers acting on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.

5. Data retention

We retain business correspondence and contact information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Inactive inquiries that do not lead to engagement are typically retained for no longer than 24 months.

6. Cookies

Our website may use minimal functional cookies necessary for the operation of the site. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking technologies.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
